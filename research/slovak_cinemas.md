# Cinemas in Slovakia with an online presence

Researched 2026-10-18. Scope: cinemas that operate today and publish a programme somewhere we could read it (own site, culture-centre site, town calendar, Facebook, or the kinoprogram.sk aggregator). Towns are sorted by number of cinemas, then alphabetically (Slovak order). Bratislava districts count as Bratislava.

Totals: 142 venues in 86 towns. The Slovak Film Institute counted 232 admission-charging cinemas at end of 2025, but 90 of those were summer cinemas, 5 travelling and 3 drive-ins; the 99 single-screen cinemas plus 28 miniplexes/multiplexes are all here except the handful listed under "Excluded" with the reason.

Legend for the tag after each link:

- **multiplex** 8+ screens, **miniplex** 2–7 screens, **single** one screen, **art** art-house or film club, **hall** culture-centre or town hall with regular public screenings, **seasonal** open-air summer cinema, **drive-in**, **travelling**.
- _(FB)_ the only live programme is on Facebook or GoOut. _(poster)_ programme published as an image or PDF. _(bot-check)_ site sits behind a Vercel JavaScript challenge and returns HTTP 429 to plain fetchers; the kinoprogram.sk mirror still has the showtimes. _(cert)_ expired or mismatched TLS certificate.

Sources: [kinoprogram.sk cinema list](https://kinoprogram.sk/kina/), [ZPK member list](https://www.zpk.sk/?page_id=74) (Združenie prevádzkovateľov kín), [SFÚ report on Slovak audiovision 2025](https://www.filmpress.sk/zaujimavosti/sprava-o-stave-slovenskej-audiovizie-v-roku-2025), [SFÚ report 2023 (PDF, cinema table p. 56–58)](https://sfu.sk/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/sprava-o-stave-slovenskej-audiovizie-2023.pdf), [cine-max.sk](https://www.cine-max.sk/), [cinemacity.sk](https://www.cinemacity.sk/), plus each cinema's own site.

## Scraping notes

- **Ticketware / mojekino.sk** is the dominant platform for town cinemas (URLs with `klient-N/kino-M/stranka-K`, or `*.mojekino.sk`). One parser should cover roughly 40 cinemas above. The hub at mojekino.sk also exposes shells for cinemas that never went live (e.g. Kino Partizán Kremnica), so an empty shell is not evidence of a cinema.
- **Vercel bot checkpoint**: about 25 of the sites (kfe.sk, nostalgia.sk, the sterio.sk and kinostar.sk subdomains, kinotatran.sk, kinomoskva.sk, gacinema.sk, kino.ruzomberok.sk and many MsKS sites) return 429 to non-browser clients. [kinoprogram.sk](https://kinoprogram.sk/kina/) mirrors showtimes for 106 of them and is the simplest fallback.
- **Cinemax**: one programme page for all 14 venues, selected by `tx_cinemax_cine[city]` (ids 1–16 above). **Cinema City**: a public quickbook JSON API keyed by cinema id. **STERIO** (ex Ster Century): Košice, Prešov, Prievidza, Spišská Nová Ves, Žilina.
- **Expired certificates** today on kinohviezda.trnava.sk, kinobardejov.sk, kino.brezno.sk, kino.levoca.sk, kinodanubius.mojekino.sk and lampart.sk.
